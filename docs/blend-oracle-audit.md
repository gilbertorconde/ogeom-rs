## Numerical and geometric limitations

The retained independent relative volume allowance is `1e-7`; the curved-crease removed-volume allowance remains `2e-6`. The former old-path relative comparison at `1e-9` is deliberately removed because that reference is not independent. The integrals use composite 8-point Gauss–Legendre on 64 panels **per smooth piece**, with no panel-agreement assertion. Each `max(0)` contact boundary is found before integration: the transverse drill uses `y=sin θ` and `z=h+cos θ` at circular-chord endpoints; the cavity starts at `z=9+√(1−(wall−4)²)`; the concave overlap uses `z=sin²θ/2`; the bored top loop uses `h=sin θ`, `y=c_y+r sin θ`, and splits at the drill/chord intersections `y=c_y±√(r²−(right−c_x)²)`. Its outer panels split where inset `d=1−cos θ` first meets the drill's near edge, the right wall meets the drill's chord extrema, the wall/chord intersections meet the moving lower y limit, and the lower limit reaches the drill's far edge. The narrow-box cap and drilled rounded block have closed forms. These splittings remove known interior branch kinks, but the remaining quadrature error has **no certified bound**; no tolerance was relaxed.

No discrepancy with a verified independent *volume* oracle remains after correcting the three expectation errors above. The five curved-crease constants remain an absolute-volume coverage gap; their order dependence is a separate suspected engine defect.
