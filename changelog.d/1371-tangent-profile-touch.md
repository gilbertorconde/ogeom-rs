- **Fixed: a loosely held fitted edge that leaves another edge tangentially from its own end is a touch, not a shared stretch** (#1371).
  - The boolean read such an edge as running along the other for as long as the two stood within the loose measuring width. It then split the other edge there, and nothing split that edge's twin on the fitted edge's own solid, so the result refused with "the kept pieces did not close into a shell". A fill fused beside a ball cut through its centre hit this: its end profile, held to 0.0057, ends on the ball's cut flat tangent to the ball's rim arc.
  - A stretch that starts at the fitted edge's own end and parts from the other edge as fast as a touch does (halfway out, at least an eighth of the final distance) is now a touch, however loosely the edge is held.
  - Checked on a closed-form fill (a torus written as a rational spline, its end profiles held to 0.005) fused beside a quarter ball on a plate: `fuse` and `fuse_seated` give valid solids whose volume and area match the closed forms to 2e-15.
