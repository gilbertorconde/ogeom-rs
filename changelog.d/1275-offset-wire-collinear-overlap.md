- **Fixed: `offset_wire` through a closing neck whose walls line up** (#1275).
  - When an inward offset closed a neck and the pieces that survived on either side lay on one line, the offset refused with "the offset's survivors close no loop". Pieces on one line or circle are now cut where the other piece ends. A shared stretch running the same way is kept once, and one running opposite ways is dropped. The rule is written up in the offsets guide.
  - Two 10-squares joined by a 4x2 neck, offset by -1.5, now give two loops. With intersection joins the area is 98.000000000 against 98. With arc joins it is 98.480162016 against 98.480157569 (bound 1e-3, sampled shoelace). Every sampled point is at the offset from the polygon within 1.2e-15. A neck exactly 3 wide also gives two loops.
