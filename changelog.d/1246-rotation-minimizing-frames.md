- **Added: `rotation_minimizing_frames`, the frames a pipe sweep carries along a curve or a wire** (#1246).
  - At any shares of the path's length in `[0, 1]`, as `CurveFrame`s (point, tangent, normal, binormal, edge and parameter), from a start normal of the caller's or the default one. The pipe sweeps now take their stations and double-reflection carrying from the same code in `ogeom_algo::frames`, so a pattern along a path and a pipe along it agree.
  - A straight run keeps its frame; an inflection needs no curvature; a planar path keeps a normal square to its plane exactly; a sharp corner of a wire turns the frame about the corner's axis by its angle (a station on the corner takes the leaving frame, the path's end the arriving one).
  - A closed path reports its holonomy as `CurveFrames::closing_twist`, not spread into the frames; `CurveFrames::closed_up` pays it off in proportion to length as the closed pipe does.
  - Helix r 10, pitch 5, three turns: twist against its closed form off by 1.2e-9 rad. Circle, line and a planar S-curve: exact to 1e-15. A skew closed polygon's frames and holonomy match the composed corner turns to 1e-15. A C2 trefoil spline: frames and holonomy within 9.1e-6 rad of the frame equation integrated on its own. Frames orthonormal to 1e-12.
