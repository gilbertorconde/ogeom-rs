- **Added: `offset::thicken_open_wire` thickens an open chain to one side or both with flat, round or extended ends, and `offset::make_rib` grows a rib from an open chain until the solid stops it** (#1242).
  - `thicken_open_wire(model, chain, plane, left, right, cap, join, tol)`: the closed outline between two offsets signed to the chain's left; `0` keeps the chain as one side. New `Cap { Flat, Round, Extended }`.
  - `make_rib(model, solid, chain, plane, thickness, along_normal, tol)`: the chain's ends are carried into the walls they point at, the web is thickened across the plane (or in it) and grown in the plane square to the chain's chord (or along the normal), and the way the solid closes is fused on. A chain that meets no wall is refused by name. Next to a wall shorter than the chain the web ends square through the chain's end and does not run round the wall.
  - **Changed:** `Join::Intersection` carries arcs on along their circles to the meeting nearest the corner, in `offset_wire` too, instead of refusing every curved side. Sides that never meet ahead of the corner are still refused by name.
  - Closed forms: a straight chain thickened one side is its 10 by 2 rectangle, an L chain 32 inside its turn and 40 (or 36 + π with arc joins) outside it, a quarter arc the annular sector 9π/4 or 11π/4; a rib across a channel adds 2 x 10 x 10, a gusset past a short wall its polygon. Areas and volumes agree to 3e-15 relative; sampled side points stand within 9e-16 of their offset.
