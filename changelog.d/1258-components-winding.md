- **Fixed: `Editable::components` keeps faces wound against each other in one piece** (#1258).
  - Pieces were followed only across the edges the joining rule joins, so a face wound the same way as a neighbour along an edge, a fin on a sheet and two bodies sharing an edge came back as separate pieces. A piece is now every face reached across shared edges, whatever the winding; faces meeting only at a point stay apart. Winding and non-manifold edges are still reported by `non_manifold` and `rims`.
  - A closed box of 12 triangles with one flipped: 1 piece of 12 (was 2), as a union-find over shared vertex pairs gives; a second, disjoint box with a flipped face: 2 pieces of 12; dropping pieces under 6 faces from the flipped box beside a tetrahedron keeps all 12 box triangles.
