- **Added: a narrow passage's band crossing a thin rib under `Overflow`** (#1237). A rib standing closer than the radius to a wall, with air behind it, is built where the rib's far wall is parallel to that wall and its edge is asked too: the wall's band crosses the gap and the rib, and what comes out behind lies within the rib's own back band, so the fill is the union of the bands. It needs `narrow_partial_face` where the rib's ends face the pocket's over part of a wall. A rib whose far side is not asked, or leans, and walls lower than the radius stay refused by name. The rule is in ADR 0001, amendment "overflow handling", part "Narrow passages, a band through a thin rib".
