- **Added: `SurfaceSheet::sheet_point`, the sheet's inverse map, and carried edges' pcurves on every face of the sheet's surface** (#1244).
  - `sheet_point(p)` takes `p` to its nearest point on the surface and returns the sheet point landing there, `None` past the reach. A plane, a cylinder and a cone are unrolled in closed form, a sphere read along the great circle from the origin, other surfaces reached by shooting geodesics, and a flattened sheet's polynomial inverted. Where several sheet points land on `p`, the one nearest the origin is returned. Refused on a pole of the chart, at a sphere's antipode and past a fold.
  - Round trips `sheet_point(lift(chart_point(s)))` against `s` measure 1e-14 mm on a plane, cylinder, cone and sphere, 8e-11 on a torus, 5e-12 on a fitted spline patch and 1e-10 through a torus's least-distortion flattening, against the 1e-9 asked.
  - `chart_edge` and `chart_edges` hang the pcurve on every face of the model whose surface is the sheet's, or an equal surface of the same kind with the same chart, so an edge running onto a sibling face splits it without its trace being fitted again.
  - **Fixed:** geodesics integrated on a spline surface stop at its knot lines, and a step's error in direction is weighed by the length still to go. A ray across a fitted patch had landed up to 13 times the accuracy asked from where it should, and at tight accuracies could not cross a knot at all. `chart_point` now lands integrated points to a thousandth of the confusion distance.
