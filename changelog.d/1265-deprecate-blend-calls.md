- **Deprecated: the option-named edge-blend calls, in favour of `BlendRequest`** (#1265).
  - Deprecated since 0.22.0, and removed in the next release: the 43 calls `fillet_edge`, `fillet_edges{,_with,_overflow,_corner,_corner_overflow,_cornered,_checked,_corner_checked,_corner_overflow_checked,_partial}`, `fillet_edge_variable`, `fillet_edges_variable{,_overflow}`, `fillet_edge_distances{,_overflow,_variable}`, `fillet_edges_distances_variable`, `chamfer_edge{,_angle,_distances}`, `chamfer_edges{,_with,_cornered,_overflow,_checked,_overflow_checked,_partial}`, `blend_edges{,_overflow}`, `largest_fillet_radius{,_all,_overflow}`, `largest_fillet_distances`, `largest_chamfer_distance{,_all,_overflow,_all_overflow}`, `largest_law_radius`, `largest_law_scale`, `largest_law_distances`, `blend_limit` and `blend_seat`; and the types `Blend`, `BlendSizing`, `LawSize` and `DistanceLawSize`.
  - Each deprecation note names the `BlendRequest` call that replaces it. They still compile, behave as before and are re-exported at `ogeom::fillet::`.
  - Migration, for the common paths: `fillet_edges(m, s, &e, r, t)` is `BlendRequest::new().round(e, r).build(m, s, t)`; `fillet_edges_corner` adds `.round_corner(c)`; `fillet_edges_variable` is `.chain(c, BlendSection::RoundLaw(law))`; `chamfer_edges_with` is one `.edge(e, spec)` per spec; `blend_edges` is one `.edge(e, blend)` per pair (`Blend` converts) then `.chamfer_corner(c)`; the `_overflow` calls add `.overflow(o)`; the `_checked` and `_partial` calls end in `.build_checked(..)` and `.build_partial(..)`; `largest_fillet_radius_all(m, s, &e, up, t)` is `.round(e, 1.0).largest(m, s, Vary::Scale, up, t)`. The full table is in `docs/design/1265-fillet-request.md`, "Migration for downstream".
  - Different on the request: the five one-edge constructions (`fillet_edge`, `chamfer_edge`, `chamfer_edge_angle`, `chamfer_edge_distances`, `fillet_edge_variable`) build through the many-edge bodies, and the checked calls `fillet_edges_checked`, `fillet_edges_corner_checked` and `chamfer_edges_checked` name every refused edge in request order instead of the first culprit and its neighbours. A request of rounds all of one radius, of no setback corners, or of one blend kind alone builds as that kind's own call, where `fillet_edges_with`, `fillet_edges_cornered` and `blend_edges{,_overflow}` used bodies of their own (ADR 0001, amendment "the public request").
