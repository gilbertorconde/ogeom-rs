- **Fixed: each end cap of a prism or a part revolution carries its own role** (#1277).
  - `make_prism` over a face and `make_revolution` through less than a full turn built both caps on the profile's own face node, and a node holds one identity, so both caps read `SWEEP_TOP` and the profile lost its own role. Each cap is now a face node of its own over the profile's surface and rings: the near cap reads `SWEEP_BOTTOM`, the far one `SWEEP_TOP`, both derived from the profile, and the profile keeps its provenance. No edge, vertex or surface is copied.
  - Checked for a plain face, a face with a hole wound either way, a profile presented backwards, a slanted travel and a 1 rad turn: the near cap's bounds match the profile's and the far cap's sit one travel along (deviation 0, bound 1e-9); the near cap faces against the travel and the far one with it (normal along the travel -1 and +1, or -0.8111 and +0.8111 for the slanted one, 5 over the square root of 38).
