- **Added: `make_thread` and `ThreadForm`, a screw thread cut on a cylindrical face; `extent_along`, a shape's exact interval along an axis** (#1247).
  - The side comes from the face: a shaft takes an external thread, a bore an internal one. The section is a trapezoid of crest, root, depth and flank angle that must close one pitch. `ThreadForm::metric` and `ThreadForm::metric_internal` give the ISO basic 60 degree forms, and `with_depth` cuts one to a given depth.
  - Right or left hand, any number of starts, a length from either end of the face, a run-out over which the depth falls linearly to nothing, and a 45 degree lead-in chamfer. Where the solid stops at the face's end the groove runs out of the material; against a shoulder it ends square in the shoulder's plane. A thread longer than its face, a chamfer against a shoulder and a non-cylindrical face are refused.
  - `extent_along` finds each end where the shape reaches it, on its exact edges and faces, as `tight_bounds` does along the world axes.
