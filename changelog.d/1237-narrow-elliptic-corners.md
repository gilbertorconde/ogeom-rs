- **Added: narrow passages through elliptic corner faces under `Overflow`** (#1237). A pocket corner or a stadium end that is an elliptic cylinder square to the floor, tighter than the radius all along, is built as the circular one is: each wall's band runs on through the corner and the fill is the union of the bands' fills, the corner face buried. It needs `narrow_whole_face` (refused by name with it and partial face off). An elliptic corner whose radius of curvature reaches the radius somewhere stays refused by name. The rule is in ADR 0001, amendment "overflow handling", part "Narrow passages, elliptic corners".
