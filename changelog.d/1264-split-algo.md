- **Changed: mesh conversion and fitting moved out of `ogeom-algo`** (#1264).
  - `docs/SCOPE.md` names the two crates that hold them, `ogeom-fit` and `ogeom-recon`. Every `ogeom::` path stays as it is.
  - `ogeom_algo::{check_wire, edge_spanned_past, volume_as_flagged, VolumeKept, self_crossings_kept, to_mesh_with, Pseudonormals}` are reachable for those crates. They are `#[doc(hidden)]` and not part of the API.
  - `Spacing`, `interpolate`, `approximate` and `approximate_within` moved down to `ogeom_geom::fit`. `ogeom::algo::` and `ogeom::algo::fit::` reach them as before, and `ogeom::geom::fit::` now does too. The `ogeom_algo::{Spacing, interpolate, approximate, approximate_within}` root paths are kept until 0.23.0, which removes them; use `ogeom_geom::fit::*`.
  - `single_precision_quantum` moved down to `ogeom_mesh`. `ogeom::algo::` and `ogeom::algo::mesh_solid::` reach it as before, and `ogeom::mesh::` now does too. The `ogeom_algo::single_precision_quantum` root path is kept until 0.23.0, which removes it; use `ogeom_mesh::single_precision_quantum`.
  - The new crate `ogeom-fit` holds the fitting: the modules `fit`, `network`, `held`, `orient`, `register`, `outline`, `segments_2d` and `rest`, and `Cutoff`, from `ogeom-algo`. Every `ogeom::algo::` path resolves as before. Direct users of `ogeom-algo` add `ogeom-fit` and rename `ogeom_algo::X` to `ogeom_fit::X` for these modules and for `Cutoff`, `NetOutside`, `net_held_within`, `Across`, `ChainedPatch`, `CurveDef`, `IrregularCorner`, `LoopSide`, `Network`, `NetworkOptions`, `PatchDef`, `PatchFit`, `PatchSide`, `fit_loop_quads`, `fit_network`, `fit_network_chained`, `Outline`, `Piece2`, `Wire2d`, `fit_outline`, `Segment2`, `Segments`, `fit_segments_2d`, `Rest`, `resting_faces` and `resting_faces_of`. The old `ogeom_algo` paths are gone: a re-export would make `ogeom-algo` depend on the crate above it.
  - The new crate `ogeom-recon` holds mesh to solid: the module `mesh_solid` from `ogeom-algo`, and `cut_by_mesh_region` from `ogeom-bool`. The module `recognize` moved from `ogeom-algo` to `ogeom-fit`. Every `ogeom::algo::` and `ogeom::boolean::` path resolves as before. Direct users rename `ogeom_algo::X` to `ogeom_recon::X` for `mesh_solid`, `FallbackReason`, `FitConstraints`, `MeshRegion`, `MeshRegions`, `MeshSolid`, `MeshSolidOptions`, `MeshSolidReport`, `RegionFallback`, `RegionId`, `RegionRefusal`, `SurfaceKind`, `refine_solid` and `solid_from_mesh`; `ogeom_algo::X` to `ogeom_fit::X` for `recognize`, `Canonical`, `Recognized` and `recognize_points`; and `ogeom_bool::cut_by_mesh_region` to `ogeom_recon::cut_by_mesh_region`. The old paths are gone, for the same reason as above. `MeshRegions::find_as_listed` is behind `ogeom-recon`'s `test-hooks` feature.
  - `ogeom_algo::weld_points` (`#[doc(hidden)]`, not part of the API) is now `ogeom_mesh::weld_points`.
  - Behaviour is unchanged.
