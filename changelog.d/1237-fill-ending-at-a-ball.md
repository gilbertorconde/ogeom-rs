- **Added: a concave edge's asymmetric fill ending at a sphere** (#1237). The fill runs on, its laws held level, and the sphere trims it, as a cylinder at the edge's end does, where the sphere spans the fill's end. A sphere that does not span it, and any end face other than a plane, a cylinder or a sphere, are refused by name. The rule is in ADR 0001, amendment "a concave fill ending at a ball".
