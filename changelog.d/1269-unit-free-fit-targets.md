- **Fixed: marched blends and drafts no longer loosen with the model's unit** (#1269).
  - The fit targets of the marched blend (two tenths of a micron) and of a drafted wall (a tenth of a micron) had a floor written in millimetres. Above 1 mm per unit the floor won: a part in inches was fitted 25.4 times looser than the same part in millimetres, one in metres 1000 times looser. Both now follow `Tolerances` alone, so nothing changes for a part in millimetres or below.
  - The margin a drafted window grows by was one model unit. It is now one millimetre in every unit. An extruded wall's growth was measured on its profile's parameter, which is no length; it is now the profile's reach in space. As a result an extruded spline wall's draft in millimetres can come out with a slightly different net: 9 by 89 poles where it was 9 by 87.
  - A leaning post's seat filleted r 1 mm states its band edges to 2.0e-4 mm in micrometres, millimetres and inches (inches was 5.08e-3 mm). A spline wall drafted 0.1 rad comes out 9 by 89 poles in micrometres, millimetres, inches and metres. It was 9 by 86 in micrometres and 4 by 49 in inches, and metres was refused as folding onto itself.
