- **Added: `face_curvature_at`, a face's curvature at the point of it nearest a given one** (#1251).
  - `ogeom::fillet::face_curvature_at(model, face, p, hint, tol)` returns a `FaceCurvature`: the principal curvatures and directions as placed and as oriented (signed as `face_curvature_samples` signs them, convex negative), the foot point, its surface parameters and its distance. Placement, scale, orientation, projection (with an optional parameter hint for continuity) and the trim are handled inside; a foot outside the face is refused with `Domain`.
  - Where the surface's chart collapses (a sphere's pole, a patch's collapsed row) the curvature is the limit approaching it; at an apex, where no limit exists, the point is refused.
  - Measured against closed forms: plane 0; cylinder r 4 turned, `0` and `-0.25` to 6e-17; sphere r 5 scaled 2, `-0.1` (`0.1` reversed) to 1.5e-9, mean to 1e-12; torus 10/3 at both equators exact; a cubic patch against finite differences of its own points to 7.4e-8; across a spline tube's seam the two sides agree to 1.7e-16.
